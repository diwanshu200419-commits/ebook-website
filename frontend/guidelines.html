Community Guidelines

These guidelines help keep E-Book Market safe, professional and valuable for creators, students and readers worldwide.

📚 Content Quality Standards

Creators must ensure that uploaded books, notes and digital products are high quality and useful for users.

  • Provide original and readable content.
  • Use proper formatting and clear titles.
  • Avoid misleading thumbnails or descriptions.
  • Ensure uploaded files work properly.

🚫 Prohibited Content

The following types of content are strictly prohibited on E-Book Market.

  • Copyrighted or pirated material.
  • Hate speech or harmful content.
  • Adult or explicit material.
  • Malware, viruses or dangerous files.
  • Fake or scam products.

⚠ Important Notice

Accounts repeatedly violating these policies may face suspension or permanent removal from the platform.

💰 Creator Responsibilities

Creators are responsible for maintaining professionalism and ethical selling practices.

  • Provide accurate product information.
  • Respond respectfully to customers.
  • Do not manipulate ratings or reviews.
  • Deliver promised digital content.

🔐 User Safety & Security

We prioritize user privacy and secure transactions across the platform.

  • Never share personal account credentials.
  • Use secure payment methods only.
  • Report suspicious activity immediately.
  • Respect privacy of creators and buyers.

🌍 Community Behavior

We encourage a supportive and positive creator ecosystem.

  • Respect all creators and users.
  • Avoid harassment or abusive behavior.
  • Support genuine learning and growth.
  • Contribute positively to the community.

Help Us Build a Better Community

By following these guidelines, you help create a trusted and professional marketplace for everyone.
