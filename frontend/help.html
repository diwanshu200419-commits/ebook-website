Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I buy a book?

Browse the marketplace, open a paid book or your cart, then contact the store team on WhatsApp or phone to confirm the order.

Can I upload and sell my own books?

Yes! Register as a creator and upload ebooks, notes or digital products from your dashboard.

How do creators get paid?

Creators receive earnings directly through supported payout methods after successful purchases.

How are paid-book orders confirmed?

This store does not take online payments on the website. Paid-book orders are confirmed through WhatsApp and the support phone number.

How can I contact support?

You can contact our support team through the Contact page or email support@ebookmarket.com
